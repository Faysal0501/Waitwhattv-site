Privacy Policy — WaitWhatTV Hub

Last updated: September 10, 2026.

WaitWhatTV Hub is a creator tool used by the account owner to prepare and manage original short-form social media content.

TikTok account data

When the user chooses to connect TikTok, TikTok may provide authorization information and basic account information required for the requested integration. WaitWhatTV Hub uses this information only to provide the TikTok connection and content-posting features requested by the authorized user.

Content posting

Videos and related captions are sent to TikTok only as part of the content-posting workflow controlled by the authorized user. During development, privacy restrictions required by TikTok are respected.

Storage

Authorization credentials used by the local WaitWhatTV Hub application are stored locally for maintaining the authorized connection and are not intentionally published in the public source repository.

Sharing

WaitWhatTV Hub does not sell TikTok account data. Information is shared with TikTok only as necessary to use TikTok's authorized APIs and perform actions requested by the user.

Access and deletion

The user can revoke TikTok authorization and stop using the integration. Locally stored authorization data can also be deleted to disconnect the account.

Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to the contact information associated with the WaitWhatTV Hub developer application.
