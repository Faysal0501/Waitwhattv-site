WaitWhatTV Hub

WaitWhatTV Hub is a creator tool used to prepare and manage original short-form videos for the WaitWhatTV social channels.

TikTok integration

The TikTok integration lets the authorized account owner connect their TikTok account and send videos created in WaitWhatTV Hub to TikTok. Posting is initiated and controlled by the authorized user. During development and testing, TikTok posts remain private/SELF_ONLY where required by TikTok.
